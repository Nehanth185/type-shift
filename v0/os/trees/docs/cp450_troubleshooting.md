Section 9: Troubleshooting (part 1)

Checks marked LOTO need the pump motor locked out and tagged out first (see page 21).
Checks marked RUN need the pump running for at least 2 minutes.

Symptom: Low discharge pressure or low flow (below 55 PSI)
| Cause | Check | Normal | Remedy |
| Suction valve partly closed | Suction valve handle in line with the pipe | Fully open | Open the suction valve fully |
| Discharge valve partly closed | Discharge valve handle in line with the pipe | Fully open | Open the discharge valve fully |
| Suction strainer clogged | RUN: strainer differential pressure | 0-3 PSI | LOTO: clean the suction strainer |
| Air leak on the suction side | RUN: bubbles in the suction sight glass | No bubbles | Tighten the suction flange bolts, replace the flange gasket |
| Wrong rotation after motor work | Motor fan turns the way of the arrow on the casing | Same as arrow | Electrician swaps two motor leads |
| Worn impeller | LOTO: impeller vane tips | No chipped or rounded tips | Replace the impeller |

Suction pressure at the suction gauge (RUN): normal 5-15 PSI. Below 5 PSI means the suction side is starved (valve, strainer or air leak).
Discharge valve handle in line with the pipe means fully open.

Section 9: Troubleshooting (part 2)

Symptom: Pump leaking
| Cause | Check | Normal | Remedy |
| Mechanical seal worn | RUN: drips per minute at the seal | 0-10 drips per minute | LOTO: replace the seal (pages 21-22) |
| Seal installed wrong | Seal replaced in the last 7 days | No | LOTO: reinstall the seal, do not touch the seal faces |
| Seal burned by dry running | RUN: casing temperature | 20-80 C | Refill and prime before restart, then replace the seal |
| Casing bolts loose | LOTO: casing bolt torque | 25-30 Nm | Tighten the four casing bolts in a cross pattern |
| Drain plug loose | Leak at the 13 mm drain plug | No leak at the plug | LOTO: tighten the drain plug, replace its washer |

Symptom: Motor trips or runs hot
| Cause | Check | Normal | Remedy |
| Motor overloaded | RUN: motor current | 18-22 A | Close the discharge valve slightly, check the system curve |
| Low supply voltage | Electrician: supply voltage | 440-500 V | Electrician fixes the supply |
| Motor fan cover clogged | LOTO: motor fan cover clear of dirt | Clean | Clean the fan cover |
| Bearing worn | RUN: bearing housing temperature | 20-70 C | LOTO: replace the bearings |

Symptom: Noise or vibration
| Cause | Check | Normal | Remedy |
| Cavitation | RUN: suction pressure | 5-15 PSI | Clear the suction side (valve, strainer) |
| Bearing worn | RUN: bearing housing temperature | 20-70 C | LOTO: replace the bearings |
| Base bolts loose | LOTO: base bolts tight | Tight | Tighten the base bolts |
| Coupling misaligned | LOTO: coupling gap | 2-4 mm | Realign the coupling |
