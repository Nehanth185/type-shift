FICTIONAL DOCUMENT written to test that the fault-tree format works on a second machine. The AC-20 compressor and every number here are made up. Do not use for real equipment.

Section 5: Safety

Lock out and tag out the compressor and bleed the tank to 0 PSI before any service work.
RUN checks need the compressor running and loaded for at least 3 minutes.

Section 6: Troubleshooting

Symptom: Tank pressure builds too slowly or never reaches 125 PSI
| Cause | Check | Normal | Remedy |
| Intake filter clogged | LOTO: intake filter element | Clean, not dark | Replace the intake filter element |
| Drain valve left open | Tank drain valve handle | Closed | Close the drain valve |
| Leaking check valve | RUN: air hissing from the unloader line when loaded | No hissing | Replace the check valve |
| Loose or cracked discharge line | Soapy water on discharge line joints | No bubbles | Tighten the joint, replace a cracked line |
| Worn piston rings | RUN: pump-up time from 90 to 125 PSI | 0-60 seconds | Replace the piston rings |

Symptom: Compressor trips on high temperature
| Cause | Check | Normal | Remedy |
| Blocked cooler fins | LOTO: cooler fins | Clear of dust | Blow out the cooler fins |
| Low oil level | Oil sight glass | Between the marks | Add oil to the mark |
| Cooling fan not turning | RUN: cooling fan | Turning | Replace the fan motor |
| Hot room | RUN: room air temperature | 40-100 F | Ventilate the room |
