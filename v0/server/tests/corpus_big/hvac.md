# RTU-20 Rooftop Unit Service Sheet (fictional)

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Low refrigerant

Suction pressure below 105 psi with icing on the coil means low refrigerant charge. Find the leak before adding R-410A. Recover and weigh in 9.5 lb.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Compressor won't start

If the contactor pulls in but the compressor hums, check the start capacitor. Replace with a 45/5 uF 440V dual-run capacitor.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Supply fan belt

Belt deflection should be 0.5 inch at mid-span. Replace belts in matched sets. Tighten motor mount bolts to 35 ft-lb.

<!-- page: 4 -->
## Filter change

Use 20x25x4 MERV 11 filters. Change every 90 days, or when pressure drop across the filter reaches 0.8 inch w.c.
