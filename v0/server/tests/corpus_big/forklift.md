# Electric Forklift FL-25 Daily Check (fictional)

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Battery

Battery electrolyte must cover the plates. Charge at 80 percent depth of discharge. Never let the voltage drop under 1.75 V per cell.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Forks

Retire forks if the heel wear exceeds 10 percent of original thickness. Check the fork angle; it should be within 3 degrees of 90.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Tire

Cushion tires: replace when worn to the wear line molded in the sidewall. Inflate pneumatic tires to 115 psi.

<!-- page: 4 -->
## Load rating

Rated capacity is 5000 lb at a 24 inch load center. Do not lift above 130 inches with the standard mast.
