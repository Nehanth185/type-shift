# Panel LP-3 Maintenance Procedure (fictional)

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Lockout before opening

De-energize breaker 12 and apply a red lock and tag. Test for absence of voltage with a CAT III meter on all three phases before touching anything.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Breaker torque

Torque 60 A breaker lugs to 25 in-lb. Torque the main lugs to 275 in-lb. Re-check after 24 hours of load.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Thermal scan

Infrared scan under at least 40 percent load. A lug more than 15 degrees C above its neighbors needs retightening within 7 days.

<!-- page: 4 -->
## Arc flash label

Incident energy at this panel is 4.2 cal/cm2 at 18 inches. Wear category 2 PPE with a face shield.
