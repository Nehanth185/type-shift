# MIG Welding Station Setup Card (fictional)

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Shielding gas

Use 75/25 argon CO2 at 25 CFH for mild steel. Check the hose for leaks with soapy water.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Wire feed

For 0.035 wire on 1/4 inch plate, set 18 volts and 350 inches per minute wire speed. Replace the drive roll if it slips.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Porosity

Pinholes in the bead usually mean gas coverage was lost. Check the nozzle for spatter and for drafts from fans.

<!-- page: 4 -->
## Fire watch

Keep a fire watch for 30 minutes after welding. Remove combustibles within 35 feet. Extinguisher is on the north wall.
