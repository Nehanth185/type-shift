## Air-in-line alarm

The air-in-line alarm means the pump sensed an air bubble in the tubing.

1. Press Pause and clamp the line below the pump.
2. Open the pump door and find the air bubble.
3. Prime the line per the unit guide to clear the air.
4. Close the door, unclamp, restart, and check the rate matches the order.

WARNING: Follow unit policy.
Call the charge nurse if the alarm repeats.

## Occlusion alarm

The occlusion alarm means something is blocking flow, often a closed clamp or a kinked line.

1. Press Pause.
2. Check every clamp on the line is open.
3. Look for kinks from the bag to the patient.
4. Restart and watch the first minute of flow.
