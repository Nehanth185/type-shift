Section 4: Operating limits

Normal discharge pressure is 55-70 PSI.
Read the pressure on the discharge gauge with the pump running for at least 2 minutes.
Below 50 PSI: check the suction strainer for a clog and look for air leaks on the suction side.
Above 75 PSI: stop the pump and check that the discharge valve is fully open.
Maximum casing temperature is 80 C. A hot casing with low flow means the pump may be running dry.

Section 6: Mechanical seal replacement (seal swap)

WARNING: Lock out the pump before service.
Lock out and tag out the pump motor before any service work.
Tools: 13 mm wrench, seal kit SK-450, drain pan, clean rag.

1. Close the suction valve and the discharge valve.
2. Drain the casing through the drain plug at the bottom (Figure 7).
3. Remove the four casing bolts and slide the motor and impeller back.

<!-- page: 22 -->
4. Remove the old seal and clean the shaft sleeve with a clean rag.
5. Install the new seal from kit SK-450. Do not touch the seal faces.
6. Reassemble, refill, open both valves, and run for 10 minutes while you check for leaks.

Figure 7: the drain plug is the 13 mm hex plug at the lowest point of the casing.
After a seal replacement, do a leak check again after 7 days of running.
